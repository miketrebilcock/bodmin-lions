We can only make grants that fit our charitable aims, and we mainly help people and groups in Bodmin and the surrounding villages. Requests are considered at our monthly meeting.

Your application comes to the club by email. It takes about 15 minutes. Have the costs to hand before you start.

Prefer paper? [Download the PDF form](/docs/grant-request-form.pdf) instead.
