The Ten Tors walk is a sponsored team walk of about **13 miles** over moorland on Bodmin Moor. It is **not a race**. In 2026 we held our 43rd walk, with 83 teams and 276 walkers, and teams raised **well over £20,000** for their chosen charities.

## Who can enter

- Walkers must be **12 or over**.
- Teams with 12 to 16-year-olds must include at least one adult.
- Every team must be sponsored. You choose the charity, or raise money for your own club's activities.
- **Please tell us how much you raise.** A perpetual trophy goes to the highest-sponsored team.

## How to enter

Entries open in January. Download the entry form from this page, fill it in on your device, save it, and email it to the address on the form. Please type your name in the signature box rather than printing and signing.

**Gift Aid:** if your charity can claim Gift Aid, make sure the full charity details are on your sponsor form.

## On the day

Bring water (there are no cups at the water stops), sun cream or waterproofs as needed, and suitable footwear. Bodmin Moor is an agricultural common, and the walk only happens thanks to the landowners and commoners. Our thanks also go to East Cornwall Search & Rescue and St Breward Football Club.
