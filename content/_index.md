The Lions Club of Bodmin was chartered on 28 April 1970 and is part of Lions Clubs International, the world's largest volunteer service organisation. Our motto is **"We Serve"**.

We are a mixed club with members from their 20s to their 90s. Members give their time for free and pay their own subscriptions, so money raised from the public goes to charitable causes — locally, nationally and internationally.

Every year we hand out cheques to groups such as Castle Canyke Scouts, St Mabyn Scouts, Bodmin Age Concern and East Cornwall Search & Rescue, and we award the Sean Whitmore Trophy to an inspiring young sportsperson.
