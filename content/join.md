We're ordinary local people doing extraordinary things together. We give our time, support our community, make friends and have a good laugh along the way. You don't need special skills or loads of spare time to start a conversation about joining us.

You don't have to become a member to help. Many people start by giving a couple of hours to one event.

## Volunteer for an event

- **Father Christmas sleigh (December)** — collectors and drivers for evening rounds across Bodmin.
- **Ten Tors walk (May)** — marshals and checkpoint helpers on Bodmin Moor.
- **Fireworks (5 November)** — set-up, gate staff and marshals in Priory Park.
- **St Piran's Day (5 March)** — marshalling the schools' parade down Fore Street.

## Become a Lion

Members meet once a month, help run events, decide together where the money goes, and get to know people across Bodmin. We welcome everyone over 18, whatever your age or background. Members pay a modest annual subscription to cover club running costs.
