Handover night on Monday 6 July at Bodmin Rugby Club marked the end of one Lion year and the start of the next. It's a chance to thank the people who've helped us, give out awards and cheques, and change club officers. This year there was no change at the top — **Bill May is staying on for another year as President.**

- Cheques went to **Castle Canyke Scouts**, **St Mabyn Scouts** and **Bodmin Age Concern**.
- The **Sean Whitmore Trophy**, for achievement and encouragement in sport for young people, was presented with a cheque. Four runners-up each received a certificate and a smaller cheque.
- A shield went to the Ten Tors team that raised the most for their charity — over £3,000.
- We also presented a cheque to **East Cornwall Search & Rescue** for their help at Ten Tors.

The Mayor of Bodmin, Councillor Mike Barbery, thanked the Lions for their work on town events.
