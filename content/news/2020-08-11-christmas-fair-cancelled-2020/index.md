After long and careful consideration, Bodmin Lions have to regrettably announce that the Christmas fair and senior citizens party, due to take place on 28th and 29th November respectively, will not take place. Bodmin lions appreciate that this will cause disappointment for many, but given circumstances and the nature of both events, we cannot put the health of the public and our members at risk. In hope and prayers that the world will soon be a safer place, we hope that all lion's events will soon resume. Bodmin Lions Christmas sleigh is still under consideration.
