- We marshalled the **Bodmin Beast Marathon** through Lanhydrock and the surrounding villages.
- Four members attended the District spring forum.
- We funded two lightweight tents for a local **explorer scout group** going to an international camp.
- We finished the month with our charter dinner at the Westbury Hotel.
