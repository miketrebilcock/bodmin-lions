The evening of Tuesday 15 July saw some disruption in Fore Street.

The text below has been copied from Bodmin council Facebook page.

pictures by Lion Keith, more pictures on the council Facebook

The Lions Club Of Bodmin and Bodmin Town Council Community Services team came together to install bunting along Fore Street, getting the town ready for a fantastic season ahead.

We’d like to say a huge thank you to everyone who gave up their free time to make this possible:

Dave, Phil, Mark, Keith, Ron, Sue, Bill, Jon, Clive

& of course... Maisy the Labrador

Your hard work and community spirit are truly appreciated!

Don’t miss what’s coming up this summer in Bodmin!

Saturday 26th July

Sports in the Park – Fun for all ages at Priory Park

Bodmin Market – Local traders, food, and crafts on Mount Folly

Alstock – A brilliant day of live music and community at Priory Park
