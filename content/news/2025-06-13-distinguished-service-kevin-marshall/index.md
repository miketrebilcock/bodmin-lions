President Dave Truran presented past President Lion Kevin Marshall with a Distinguished Service Plaque. Kevin joined Bodmin Lions Club in 2003 and was President three times and held many roles over the years.

He was Chairman of the Christmas Fayre, Senior Citizens Party, and is currently Chairman of Bodmin St Pirans Day Committee. He also spent many years calling at our weekly bingo evenings and is our Health & Safety Officer
