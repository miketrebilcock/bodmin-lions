- [Lions Clubs International](https://www.lionsclubs.org/) — the international headquarters.
- [Lions Clubs International Foundation (LCIF)](https://www.lionsclubs.org/en/discover-our-foundation/mission) — co-ordinates large Lions aid projects.
- [Lions Clubs British Isles](https://lionsclubs.co.uk/) — the UK and Ireland headquarters.
- [Lions District 105SW](https://www.lions105sw.org.uk/) — Lions in the South West.
- Message in a Bottle — the free Lions scheme that helps emergency services find your medical details at home. Ask us for a bottle.
- [Bodmin Town Council](https://www.bodmin.gov.uk/)
