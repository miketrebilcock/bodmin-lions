description: How Bodmin Lions Club handles personal information.
---
Bodmin Lions Club (a Charitable Incorporated Organisation, charity number 1178036) is responsible for the personal information described here.

## This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which handles visitors' IP addresses and keeps short-lived technical logs so it can deliver the site and protect it from attack. We don't use these to identify anyone.

## If you email us

We use your details only to reply and to organise the activity you asked about. We don't share them with anyone else.

## If you apply for a grant

Your application comes to the club's email account. If you use the online form, it passes through Cloudflare on the way, but the website itself doesn't store your answers.

We use your application only to consider it and, if it succeeds, to administer the grant. Our trustees may share it with other people or organisations where that is needed to consider it. If you agreed on the form, we may publicise a grant you receive, including photos or video of the presentation.

We keep applications only as long as we need them to decide on and administer the grant.

## Ten Tors entries

Entry forms are kept only as long as needed to run the event and meet our insurance requirements.

## Your rights

To ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, email [bodmin.lions@outlook.com](mailto:bodmin.lions@outlook.com). If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
