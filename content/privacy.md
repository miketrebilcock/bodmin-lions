*Draft for the club to review.*

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. If you email us, we use your details only to reply and to organise the activity you asked about, and we don't share them with anyone else. Ten Tors entry forms are kept only as long as needed to run the event and meet our insurance requirements.

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email [bodmin.lions@outlook.com](mailto:bodmin.lions@outlook.com).
