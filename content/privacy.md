description: How Bodmin Lions Club handles personal information.
---
*Draft for the club to review.*

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. If you email us, we use your details only to reply and to organise the activity you asked about, and we don't share them with anyone else. If you apply for a grant using the online form, your answers are sent to the club by email through Cloudflare, which hosts this website. The website itself doesn't store your answers. We keep grant applications only as long as we need them to decide on and administer the grant.

Ten Tors entry forms are kept only as long as needed to run the event and meet our insurance requirements.

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email [bodmin.lions@outlook.com](mailto:bodmin.lions@outlook.com).
