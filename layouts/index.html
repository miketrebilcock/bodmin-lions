Ordinary local people doing extraordinary things for Bodmin

We're your local Lions Club, here since 1970. We run the Ten Tors walk on Bodmin Moor, take Father Christmas round the town every December, help put on the Priory Park fireworks — and every penny we raise from the public goes to good causes.
